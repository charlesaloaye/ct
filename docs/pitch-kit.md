# Pitch kit: Charles Aloaye

Ready-to-send pitches for tech publications and podcasts. Everything here is factual. Text in [square brackets] is for you to fill before sending, such as the editor's name. Never send a pitch with brackets left in.

## How to use it

1. **Pick one story per outlet.** Don't send the same email to everyone; editors talk to each other.
2. **Find the right person.** Use each outlet's own "contact", "tips" or "contribute" page, or the author of a recent article on a similar topic. Don't guess email addresses.
3. **Keep it short:** the subject line, three short paragraphs and a sign-off.
4. **Follow up once** after 5–7 working days, then move on.
5. **Log it:** when something is published, add it in **Admin → Media & press** with the link.

## Story angles

Each angle is a different reason for an editor to care.

1. **Building education tools for Nigerian students.** One Lagos studio, Built Different, runs DelsuTools, UniAdvisor, JAMB Advisor, StudyAbroad.ng and ScholarshipHQ.ng. Together they cover a student's path from UTME to university to studying abroad.
2. **Frontstore: online selling for small businesses.** A store in minutes, orders and checkout inside WhatsApp, payments and delivery in one place.
3. **Rescuing abandoned software.** A practice built on taking over stalled codebases and getting them to launch, usually without a rewrite.
4. **Engineer and certified teacher.** A TRCN-registered educator who builds software and also teaches developers, including a full-stack curriculum he built and taught.
5. **AI agents in the real world.** Making an Apify Instagram analyzer safe for AI agents to call, and wiring it into Slack over MCP, as published by Apify on DEV.

Strengthen any angle with a real number you can stand behind: users, students helped, launch dates. Leave numbers out rather than estimate.
